Words in Context Try it

Astronomer Vera Rubin's measurements of galaxy rotation didn't merely refine existing models of the universe; they ______ them, forcing physicists to accept that most of the universe's mass is invisible.

Which choice most logically and precisely completes the text?

The sentence sets up an escalation: not merely refine … but ______. So the blank has to be something bigger than refining.

Then the second half restates it for you — physicists were forced to accept something entirely new. That's the context clue. Upended is the only choice stronger than "refine." Supplemented and echoed are weaker, and predicted changes the meaning altogether.
