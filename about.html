About

From 1280 to 1520, and what I learned in between.

2 min read

I took the SAT officially twice, both times in twelfth grade. My first score was a 1280. My second was a 1520.

The jump didn't come from a trick. It came from a plan: two months, three hours a day, two full-length practice tests every week, and reviewing my mistakes in Bluebook. My approach was always to just solve more questions — and then figure out why I got the wrong ones wrong.

The resources were all free: Bluebook for practice tests, Khan Academy for practice questions, and James Lu's Desmos videos on YouTube for math.

Why this site exists

Most SAT advice is either too vague to act on or buried inside something trying to sell you a course. I wanted to write down exactly what worked, including the parts that aren't glamorous, and make it free.

Everything here is either what I actually did or the methods I used for each question type. If you're starting where I started, this is the path I took.
